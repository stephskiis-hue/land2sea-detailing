Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

1. Who We Are

Land2Sea Detailing is a mobile and in-shop car detailing service based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. We can be reached at kaidenadolf@gmail.com or by phone at 204-558-1791.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request through our website, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Vehicle information
  • Your message or service request

We do not collect payment information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your quote request or inquiry
  • Schedule and confirm your detailing appointment
  • Contact you about your service

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site. GA4 may set cookies in your browser and collects anonymized usage data such as pages visited and time on site. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data Retention

Quote request emails are retained in our inbox only as long as necessary to fulfill your service request. We do not maintain a separate database of customer personal information.

6. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at kaidenadolf@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at kaidenadolf@gmail.com or call 204-558-1791.
